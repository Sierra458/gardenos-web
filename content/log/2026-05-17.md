**Transplant timing observations:**
Tomatoes and peppers should be 8–12" tall with first flowers by May 17 in Houston Zone 9a. Current measurements: 3–5" with no flowers. They're behind schedule — recoverable with weekly fish emulsion feedings and consistent water, but every week now is stolen from peak harvest window before the 90°F+ wall in mid-June kills fruit set on tomatoes.

**Mulch-on-stem issue across raised bed:**
On close inspection, straw mulch is piled too close to stems on several tomatoes and BP2. Houston humidity + mulch contact = stem rot risk and pest harborage. Universal fix: pull mulch back 2" from every stem.

**Onion wrong-season reality:**
Houston onions want October–January sowing for May–June harvest. April-sown onions in a Zone 9a summer don't bulb properly. The four current shoots will be harvested as scallions in 4–6 weeks, then the space reused. Proper onion sets (small bulbs) get planted in October — variety should be **Texas Grano 1015Y** (short-day, perfect for Houston).

**Lavender mixed performance:**
Two clumps flowering well, two clumps showing heavy dieback. Likely root rot from Houston humidity + possibly residual fertilizer salts. Treatment: aggressive prune back to silver/green wood, stop watering for a week, stop fertilizing entirely. Lavender is a 1–2 year crop in Houston regardless — consider replacing failing clumps with rosemary (same look, actually thrives here).

**Carrot bucket repurposing:**
The 5-gallon orange Home Depot bucket has good soil. Best replacement crops for late-May Houston planting: okra (single plant fills the bucket, loves heat), bush beans (60-day harvest), or a sweet potato slip (huge tuber yield).

## 🚨 Issues

- **Lime tree pest pressure** — citrus leaf miner on new flush (cosmetic, tree outgrows it but reduces vigor) + slugs climbing at night. Treatment plan: weekly neem oil on new growth, iron phosphate bait (Sluggo) around pot base, copper tape barrier on pot rim, thin straw mulch around trunk.
- **Tomato/pepper growth lag** — recoverable but needs immediate intervention with fish emulsion + Myco Supreme drench if not already done.
- **Lavender dieback** — half the planter is failing. Half-save the rest with aggressive pruning + drought regimen.
- **Carrot crop lost** — full season miss. Replant proper carrots Sept 15 for fall harvest.
