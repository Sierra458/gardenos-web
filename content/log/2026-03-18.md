## 📝 Notes

**Key decisions:**
- [[Raspberry Pi 5]] = central hub (indoors), [[Arduino Mega]] = outdoor sensor controller
- [[ESP Display 5in]] chosen as primary display over [[GIGA Display Shield]] (self-contained WiFi, no extra board needed)
- Pi 1B boards shelved — Pi 5 handles everything
- Single pump + 4 solenoid valves for per-zone control
- Sensor 1 → Lavender (prevent overwatering), Sensor 2 → Potato tower (prevent underwatering)
- Mint & citronella on time-based schedules until Phase 2 sensors arrive

**Critical finding — Potato tower in Houston:**
- ⚠️ When soil temps exceed 75°F, potatoes stop forming tubers
- Houston summer soil temps easily hit 90°F+ in direct sun
- Spring planting must be harvested by late May
- Fall planting (late Aug/Sep) is actually better — crop matures as temps cool
- Consider shade cloth and soil temperature monitoring

**Lavender in Houston — survival tips:**
- Don't expect more than 2–3 years of life
- Mulch with gravel/expanded shale, NOT organic mulch
- The system must PREVENT overwatering — biggest killer in Houston humidity
- Best varieties: Sweet Lavender, Fern Leaf, French/Spanish types

**Blockers:**
- ⚠️ No water pump, solenoid valves, or tubing yet — need to order
- ⚠️ ESP display exact model still unconfirmed
- No weatherproof enclosure yet
