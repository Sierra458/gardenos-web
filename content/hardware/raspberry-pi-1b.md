## Specs

- **SoC:** BCM2835 (ARM1176JZF-S @ 700 MHz)
- **RAM:** 512 MB
- **Connectivity:** 10/100 Ethernet, no onboard WiFi/BT
- **GPIO:** 26-pin header
- **Quantity:** 2 units

## Possible Roles

- Lightweight sensor relay nodes (with USB WiFi dongle)
- Serial bridge to Arduino in a remote garden zone
- May be too underpowered — evaluate during prototyping

## Limitations

- No WiFi/BT onboard (needs dongle)
- Very limited RAM — use Raspberry Pi OS Lite or DietPi
