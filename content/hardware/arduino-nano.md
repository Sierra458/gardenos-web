> Compact secondary sensor node for [[Garden Monitor]].

## Specs

- **MCU:** ATmega328P
- **Digital I/O:** 22 pins (6 PWM)
- **Analog Inputs:** 8
- **Flash:** 32 KB · **Clock:** 16 MHz
- **USB:** Mini-B
- **Purchased:** Dec 2, 2025

## Role in Project

- Secondary or remote sensor node (e.g., second garden bed)
- Small enough to embed in a weatherproof housing
- Communicates with [[Raspberry Pi 5]] via serial or wireless add-on
