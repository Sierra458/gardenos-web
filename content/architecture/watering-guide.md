> Per-plant watering schedules for the [[Garden Monitor]] indoor nursery and outdoor garden. Houston (Zone 9a, southwest-facing patio).

## The Universal Rule

Soil should feel like a **wrung-out sponge** — damp throughout but never sitting in water. Stick your finger ¼" into the soil. If it feels dry, water. If it's still moist, leave it alone.

## Indoor Nursery — Seedling Stage

| Tray / Location | Frequency | Method | Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plastic cells (Shelves 1–3)** | Every 1–2 days | Spray bottle / gentle mist | 1–2 Tbsp per cell, until surface evenly damp |
| **Big container (Shelf 4)** | Every 1–2 days, water when top ¼" dry | Mist or gentle pour | More volume, deeper soak |
| **Egg carton (Shelf 4) — eggshell halves** | **Once daily** (sealed eggshells retain moisture) | Mist | **1–2 tsp max per shell** — easy to overwater |
| **Cardboard egg carton without shells** | **Twice daily** (cardboard wicks fast) | Mist | Just enough to dampen cardboard visibly |

### When True Leaves Appear (~Week 2–3)

Switch to **bottom watering**:

- Pour water into the black catch tray.
- Let cells wick moisture from below for ~20 min.
- **Dump any standing water** afterward.
- Encourages roots to grow downward + prevents damping-off (fungal seedling killer).

### Don't Do

- ❌ Pour water directly onto seeds or tiny sprouts (dislodges them, compacts soil).
- ❌ Leave standing water in catch trays >30 min (root rot, damping-off).
- ❌ Mist top of eggshells if water pools and doesn't absorb in 1 minute (overwatering — shells don't drain).
- ✅ **Tip:** Pin a tiny hole in the bottom of each eggshell for drainage.

### Heat Adjustments

Warm shelves (3–4) dry faster than the cool bottom (lettuce). PVC cover helps retain humidity. **Crack open the cover for 30 min daily** for air circulation — prevents mold without dropping humidity too far.

## Outdoor — Per Plant

### Raised Bed (Pre-Transplant)

- **Frequency:** Every 2–3 days while empty (just keep soil moist for transplant readiness).
- Mulch covers everything; pull aside at planting spots when transplant day comes.

### Tomatoes (Raised Bed) — Once Transplanted

- **Frequency:** Deep soak every 2–3 days; daily during establishment week.
- **Method:** At the base, never on leaves (Houston humidity + wet foliage = fungal disaster).
- **Time:** Morning only. Evening watering leaves wet leaves overnight.
- **Volume:** Until water runs out the bottom of the bed. Shallow watering = shallow roots = stressed plants in summer.

### Peppers + Jalapeños (Raised Bed)

- Same as tomatoes; slightly more drought-tolerant. Every 3 days fine in mild weather; daily in 95°F+ stretches.

### Onions (Raised Bed)

- 1–2× per week. Don't overwater — onions rot in soggy soil. Surface dries between waterings is fine.

### Basil (Raised Bed)

- More frequent than tomatoes; basil wilts visibly when thirsty. Every 1–2 days. Recovers fast after a soak.

### Corn (Circular Containers)

- **Establishment week (post-transplant):** Deep soak every morning. Until water runs out the bottom drain holes.
- **Established (~week 2+):** Stick finger 2" into soil under the straw. If dry at that depth, water. Daily in May/June heat.
- **At tasseling:** Don't let it dry out — pollination is moisture-sensitive.
- **Time:** Morning only.

### Watermelon (Container with Trellis)

- **Establishment week:** Deep daily watering.
- **Established:** Stick finger 2" deep under straw. Likely **daily, sometimes twice daily** above 95°F. Watermelon is **92% water by weight** — needs constant supply for fruit.
- **At fruit set:** Keep watering **consistent**. Erratic watering causes cracking + hollow heart.
- **One week before harvest:** Reduce to every other day. Slight stress concentrates sugars = sweeter fruit.
- **Method:** Base only, slow trickle under the straw.
- **Time:** Morning only.
- **Harvest signs:** Tendril nearest the fruit turns brown; belly spot turns from white to yellow.

### Lavender Box (Existing Herb Stand)

- **Frequency:** Once a week max once established. **Drier the better.**
- **Method:** Base only — wet foliage in Houston humidity = black leaves and death.
- **Cooldown:** Minimum 4–5 days between waterings — enforced in [[Outdoor Watering System]] firmware.
- "If you think it needs water, wait until tomorrow." (Texas lavender growers' rule.)

### Citronella (Existing Herb Stand)

- **Frequency:** Every 2–3 days in summer. Top inch dry = water.
- Drains well — gets root rot from standing water.
- The straw mulch sits up against the stem currently — **pull it back ~1"** to prevent stem rot.
- Some lower leaf yellowing is normal as new growth pushes (or could be overwatering — check drainage).

### Spearmint (Existing Herb Stand)

- **Frequency:** Daily or every other day in Houston summer. Mint is moisture-loving.
- Currently showing stress (yellowing leaves, wilting on tall stems) — likely **root-bound** in the small pot OR **too much afternoon sun**.
- **Fix options:** Upsize the pot, water more often, or relocate to partial shade. Mint goes "summer dormant" in Houston and rebounds in fall — accept the seasonal hit if relocation isn't feasible.

### Lime Tree (Citrus Pot)

- **Frequency:** Deep soak when top 2" of soil is dry — usually every 4–7 days depending on heat.
- Straw mulch in place is doing good work.
- Will need a 15–20 gallon upgrade within 1–2 years.

### Potato Tower (Hamper with Soil/Hay Layers)

- **Frequency:** Every 2–3 days. Tower dries faster than ground planting (elevated, exposed).
- **Method:** Insert a PVC pipe with holes into the tower center for **even deep watering** (best practice for tower growing). Or surface water at base + sides.
- **At tuber formation (6–8 weeks after planting):** Increase to every 1–2 days.
- **Stop watering** when vines yellow + die back (curing phase, ~2 weeks before harvest).
- **Soil temp warning:** Above 75°F, potatoes stop forming tubers. Houston spring planting must be harvested by **late May**. Fall planting (late Aug/Sept) actually works better.

### Carrot (5-Gallon Bucket)

- **Frequency:** Keep soil consistently moist until germination (10–14 days). After: every 2–3 days, deep enough to reach the bottom.
- **Don't let the soil crust** at the surface — break it up gently if it does (sealed surface stops germination).

### Oregano + Rosemary (Terracotta Pots, Once Transplanted)

- **Rosemary:** Less is more. Once a week in summer; lavender-like discipline. Hates wet feet.
- **Oregano:** A bit more than rosemary. Every 4–5 days in summer.

## Time of Day — Always Morning

- Morning watering = soil absorbs before midday heat.
- Evening watering = wet soil + Houston humidity overnight = fungal pressure.
- Aim for: **6:30–8:30 AM**.

## Don't-Do List

- ❌ Water leaves (always at the base).
- ❌ Evening watering in summer.
- ❌ Standing water in catch trays > 30 min.
- ❌ Watering tomatoes inconsistently (causes blossom-end rot — also see eggshell calcium trick in [[Indoor Nursery]] / [[Transplant Timeline]]).
- ❌ Skipping mulch (water frequency doubles without it).

- Per-zone cooldowns (lavender = 4–5 days minimum, mint = daily allowed).
- Time-window enforcement (morning only).
- Heat-aware delays (skip if ambient > 100°F at watering time, retry in evening if temps drop).
- Per-zone duration tuned from manual observations on this guide.
