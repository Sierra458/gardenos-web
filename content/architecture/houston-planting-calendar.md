> Zone 9a (Houston, TX) seed-by-seed planting decisions for the [[Garden Monitor]] system. Last frost mid-February; March is the most valuable planting window of the year.

## Plant RIGHT NOW (Direct Seed Outdoors)

- **Okra (Clemson Spineless)** — Houston's #1 summer crop. 1" deep, 12–18" apart. Soak seeds overnight first. Harvest in 55–65 days, produces straight through October.
- **Cucumber** — 1" deep, 36" apart (sprawls). Trellis or cage saves space. Inconsistent moisture causes bitter fruit. Harvest 50–70 days.
- **Corn (Sweet)** — Direct seed when soil temp hits 60°F. Plant in 4×4 blocks minimum (wind-pollinated). 1" deep, 10–12" apart. Not practical container-only.
- **Watermelon / Cantaloupe** — Hill mounds, 2–3 seeds per hill, 1" deep. 6–8 ft per vine sprawl. Compact varieties for containers. 80–90 days to fruit.
- **Basil (Genovese & Lemon)** — Barely cover seeds (need light to germinate). 12" apart. Pinch flower buds for season-long leaf production.

## Start Seeds Indoors NOW (Transplant in 3–4 Weeks)

- **Tomato** — Heat mat 70–80°F, ¼" deep. 6–8 weeks to transplant size. Mid-May transplant is workable but tight before summer heat. For spring tomatoes from store transplants instead, plant the seeds for fall (transplant in July).
- **Pepper / Jalapeño Gigante** — Same as tomato. 75–85°F soil. More heat-tolerant than tomatoes; produces well into Houston fall.
- **Rosemary** — Slow germinator (14–21 days). 70°F soil. Perennial in Houston once established. Excellent drainage required — hates wet feet.

## Plant Now But Manage Expectations

- **Lettuce (Little Gem & Gourmet Blend)** — Tail end of season. Will bolt mid-April–May. Better as a hydroponics crop or fall replant in September. Indoor in cool zone for now.
- **Cilantro** — Bolts fast at 80°F+. Maybe 3–4 weeks of harvest. Let some flower for coriander seed. Save for fall.
- **Carrot** — Direct sow OK now, but they prefer cooler soil. Deep container with loose mix, barely below surface, 10–14 days to germinate.

## Save for Fall (August–September Start)

- **Broccoli** — Cool-season. Spring start = May transplant = bolts before heading. Start indoors August, transplant September.
- **Onion (Sweet Spanish)** — Onion seed needs October–January start in Houston. Buy onion sets (small bulbs) for spring.

## Already Planted / Perennials

- **Spearmint** and **Lavender** — Established outdoors. Apply Myco Supreme to root zone now. Mycorrhizal fungi dramatically improve nutrient/water uptake (especially for lavender).

## My Recommended Allocation

- **Outdoor containers:** Okra, both basils, cucumber.
- **Indoor nursery:** Tomato, pepper, jalapeño, rosemary, lettuce, cilantro.
- **Hydroponics (future):** Lettuce (Gourmet Blend).
- **Potato tower:** Need actual seed potatoes (not from packet) — get from nursery.
- **Hold for fall:** Broccoli, onion seed, carrot, second lettuce/cilantro round.
- **Space-dependent:** Corn (4×4 minimum), watermelon (6+ ft sprawl), cantaloupe — patio-only is a tough grow.
